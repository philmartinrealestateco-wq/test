Why it appeared in our target list

out/master_targets.csv holds this address at $74,989.45 VERIFIED (USDT leg, Ethereum) — inside the $50k–$900k window.

But the same address holds 55.2M USDT today. Exchange hot wallets sweep customer deposits in and out continuously, so their spot balance crosses any fixed band briefly. The scan captured a low-balance moment and read it as a private holding. A value window cannot distinguish an individual from exchange working capital — that requires the label and the funding pattern, which is what this trace adds.

Method and limits

  • Sources: Etherscan v2 txlist / tokentx (chainid 1, ascending) for indexing, publicnode JSON-RPC for code, balances, nonces and block timestamps, and DuckDuckGo for the third-party labels.
  • Labels are third-party claims (Etherscan, Arkham), not proof — though the 300M USDT seed from a labelled Bybit wallet and 7.4M outgoing transactions corroborate the exchange reading.
  • Totals are a lower bound: the free Etherscan tier caps a page at 1,000 rows, and this wallet has 1.4M outgoing transactions, so any funding after the first 1,000 rows is not counted here.
  • Depth: two hops back. 0x9696…6976 and 0x20c2…ED7E were not traced further; both are EOAs, so a contract-deployment signature is not available to attribute them.
  • Balances are point-in-time (2026-11-15) and move constantly for a hot wallet.
